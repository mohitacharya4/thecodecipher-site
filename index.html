Tabulo

Multi-tab calculator

Keep several calculations open side by side and use one tab’s result in another. Change a number and everything linked to it updates.

  • Linked results between tabs
  • Exact decimal math: 0.1 + 0.2 = 0.3
  • Undo anything, even after clearing
  • No ads, no account, works offline

Optipixa

Photo compressor

Shrinks photos to the exact size an online form asks for, like “350×450 px, under 50 KB”, right on your phone. Nothing is uploaded.

  • Set a max size in KB, get the best quality that fits
  • Presets for passport photos, signatures and A4
  • Crop, rotate and compress up to 50 photos at once
  • No ads, no account, works fully offline
